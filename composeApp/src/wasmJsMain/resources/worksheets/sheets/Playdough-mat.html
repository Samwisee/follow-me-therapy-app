Playdough mat

Fine motor

Name

Roll a long snake and lay it on each line. Then roll ten little balls, one for each circle.

For grown-ups: slip this sheet into a plastic sleeve so the playdough wipes off. Squeezing, rolling and pinching build strength in the small muscles of the hand. A few minutes before writing or cutting works well.
