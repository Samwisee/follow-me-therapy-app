Tying shoelaces

Self-care

Name

Say it, then do it. Colour in each number when it's done.

  1. Cross the laces
  2. Tuck one under and pull tight
  3. Make a bunny ear
  4. Wrap the other lace around it
  5. Push it through the hole
  6. Pull both ears tight
Draw your shoes!

My tricky bit is…

For grown-ups: practise on a shoe on the table first, not on a foot. Two different coloured laces make each side easy to follow. Teach one step at a time and say the same words each time, so they become a little script.
