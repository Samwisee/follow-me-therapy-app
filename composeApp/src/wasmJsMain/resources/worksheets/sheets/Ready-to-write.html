Start at the gold dot and follow the arrow. Trace the grey shapes, then draw your own.

For grown-ups: these are the shapes letters are made of. Sit at a table with feet flat on the floor, and let your child trace with a finger first, then a crayon or pencil. Five minutes is plenty; praise the effort, not the neatness.
