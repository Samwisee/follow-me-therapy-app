Start at the gold dot and follow the arrow. Trace the grey letters, then write your own.

For grown-ups: these are the letter shapes Victorian schools teach. Show where the gold dot is and follow the arrow together first. Sit at a table, feet flat, and let your child trace slowly; then they write one on their own in the space at the end. A few letters a day is plenty.
